Frequently Asked Questions

How long does shipping take?
Standard shipping takes 5-7 business days. Express shipping is available and takes 2-3 business days. International orders may take up to 14 days.
What is your return policy?
We accept returns within 30 days of delivery. Items must be unworn, unwashed, and with original tags. Return shipping is free for all orders.
Do you ship internationally?
Yes, we ship to over 50 countries worldwide. International shipping costs are calculated at checkout based on destination and weight.
